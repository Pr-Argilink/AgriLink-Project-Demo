# AgriLink Constitution

The permanent rules of the AgriLink platform.

## Core Rules

1. Field is immutable.
2. Only two business actors exist: NeedOriginator and NeedExecutor.
3. No AI Agent has autonomous authority.
4. The Orchestrator is the only coordinator.
5. Business state changes only through Events.
6. CQRS is mandatory.
7. Inventory is the single source of truth for Executor capabilities.
8. Matching must be explainable.
9. Documentation is the Single Source of Truth.
